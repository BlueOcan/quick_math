Introduction

Welcome to mathVIBE ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

1

Information We Collect

mathVIBE is designed to work primarily offline. We collect minimal data:

  • Local app data — Game scores, coins, and settings stored only on your device using local storage. We never upload this to any server.
  • Ad data — Google AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data to serve relevant advertisements. See Google's privacy policy for details.

✓  We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any personal identifiers.

2

How We Use Information

The limited data we interact with is used only to:

  • Provide and improve the app experience
  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Diagnose crashes and fix bugs via anonymised crash reports
3

Third-Party Services

mathVIBE uses the following third-party service that has its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — Advertising platform. View policy

We do not sell, trade, or share your information with any other third parties.

4

Children's Privacy

mathVIBE is suitable for all ages including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Advertising shown to users designated as children is limited to child-directed ads as per Google's policies.

5

Data Storage & Security

All game progress, coins, and settings are stored locally on your device using Flutter's SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

We implement reasonable security measures but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.
